The CovidHub ensemble predicts that for the week ending September 05, 2026, the weekly percentage of new emergency department (ED) visits due to COVID-19 in the United States will be 0.64% (95% prediction interval 0.35-0.96%). The median forecast suggests an increase from 0.5%, which was reported for the week ending August 22, 2026 (the most recent week of reporting).

Ensemble forecasting also predicts that the number of new laboratory-confirmed COVID-19 hospital admissions in the United States during the week ending September 05, 2026 will be approximately 3200 (2000-4900). The median forecast suggests an increase compared to the 2500 admissions so far reported for the week ending August 22, 2026.

Overview: Forecasted percent of ED visits and new hospital admissions due to COVID-19. This week, 3 modeling groups contributed 3 forecasts of ED visits and 8 modeling groups contributed 9 forecasts of hospital admissions that were eligible for inclusion in the ensemble forecasts for at least one jurisdiction.

What does the figure show?: The figure shows the percentage of COVID-19 emergency department visits each week reported from October 01, 2022 through August 22, 2026, number of new laboratory-confirmed COVID-19 hospital admissions reported in the United States each week from August 08, 2020 through August 22, 2026, and forecasted new COVID-19 emergency department visits and hospital admissions per week for this week and the next 2 weeks through September 12, 2026.

The following jurisdictions had <80% of hospitals reporting for the most recent week: Alaska, Arkansas, Arizona, California, Colorado, District of Columbia, Florida, Georgia, Hawaii, Iowa, Idaho, Illinois, Indiana, Kansas, Kentucky, Louisiana, Maryland, Maine, Missouri, Mississippi, Montana, Nebraska, Nevada, New York, Oklahoma, Oregon, Pennsylvania, Puerto Rico, Rhode Island, South Dakota, Texas, United States, Vermont, West Virginia, and Wyoming.
