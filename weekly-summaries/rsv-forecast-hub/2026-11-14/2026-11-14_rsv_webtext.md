The RSVHub ensemble predicts that for the week ending November 21, 2026, the weekly percentage of new emergency department (ED) visits due to RSV in the United States will be 0.2% (95% prediction interval 0.1-0.3%). The median forecast suggests an increase from 0.12%, which was reported for the week ending November 07, 2026 (the most recent week of reporting).

Ensemble forecasting also predicts that the number of new laboratory-confirmed RSV hospital admissions in the United States during the week ending November 21, 2026 will be approximately 2000 (1200-3000). The median forecast suggests an increase compared to the 1200 admissions so far reported for the week ending November 07, 2026.

Overview: Forecasted percent of ED visits and new hospital admissions due to RSV. This week, 6 modeling groups contributed 7 forecasts of ED visits and 12 modeling groups contributed 14 forecasts of hospital admissions that were eligible for inclusion in the ensemble forecasts for at least one jurisdiction.

What does the figure show?: The figure shows the percentage of RSV emergency department visits each week reported from October 01, 2022 through November 07, 2026, number of new laboratory-confirmed RSV hospital admissions reported in the United States each week from October 07, 2023 through November 07, 2026, and forecasted new RSV emergency department visits and hospital admissions per week for this week and the next 2 weeks through November 28, 2026.

The following jurisdictions had <80% of hospitals reporting for the most recent week: Alaska, Arkansas, California, Colorado, District of Columbia, Florida, Georgia, Hawaii, Iowa, Idaho, Illinois, Kansas, Kentucky, Louisiana, Maryland, Minnesota, Missouri, Mississippi, Montana, North Dakota, Nebraska, New Mexico, Nevada, New York, Oklahoma, Oregon, Pennsylvania, Puerto Rico, Texas, United States, Virginia, Vermont, and Wyoming.
